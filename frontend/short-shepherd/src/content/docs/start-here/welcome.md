**CatShark TreeHouse** is a network of adult furry chats and channels spread
across Telegram and Discord, the continuation of the community previously
known as the _Zuri Cat Tree_ / _FurrK Network_. Same tree, new treehouse.

First of all: a warm **welcome** to you.

:::caution[Adults Only]
We are a series of chats that do not accept minors in our spaces **at all**,
be it SFW or NSFW ([§ 1](/rules/network-rules/#1-18-only)). This is an **active** decision made by all of us, given the
nature of adult spaces. It is final and not going to change.
:::

## Where to Go from Here

1. Read the [Network Rules](/rules/network-rules/). Admins are instructed
   not to reply to join requests from people who haven't acknowledged them.
2. Follow the [joining process](/start-here/joining/), including age
   verification ([§ 1.2](/rules/network-rules/#12-verification-required)).
3. Check the [Banned & Controlled lists](/controlled/) before posting art or
   stickers ([§ 13](/rules/network-rules/#13-respect-the-controlled-content-lists)).
4. Questions? The [FAQ](/community/faq/) or any of the
   [staff](/community/staff/) can help.
