So you want in? Great. Here's how it works.

:::caution[Read the rules first]
Go read the [Network Rules](/rules/network-rules/) before you do anything
else. Admins are instructed to **not reply** to you if you try to join without
acknowledging them first.
:::

## Age Verification

Every space in the network is 18+ ([§ 1](/rules/network-rules/#1-18-only)), so we require some form of
documentation to enter ([§ 1.2](/rules/network-rules/#12-verification-required)). We do **not** save your documentation in any way, nor would we ever
want to. The only way documentation remains visible to us is if you decide not
to delete it in a chat yourself.

## The Steps

1. Request to join a chat through the join button.
2. Talk to any of our admins. The [staff page](/community/staff/) shows who
   they are and **who can verify ID**. On Telegram,
   [Zuri](https://t.me/ZuriTheChee) (one of the owners) handles most
   verifications, so messaging him directly is the fastest route. On Discord,
   open a ticket in the server instead; the ticket system walks you through
   it.
3. Make sure you tell them you are seeking to join a specific chat.
4. Send your ID: a driving licence or national ID showing your date of birth
   ([§ 1.5](/rules/network-rules/#15-accepted-forms-of-id)). No ID at hand?
   A trusted member of the network can vouch for you instead
   ([§ 1.3](/rules/network-rules/#13-attestation)): either tell the admin
   who is vouching for you, or have that member message the admin directly.
5. Wait for instructions from the admin. You may be accepted into the group or
   asked for some further proof of documentation.
6. Once you're in, you will **not** be required to send your ID again when
   joining other groups in the network ([§ 1.4](/rules/network-rules/#14-network-wide-verification)).
