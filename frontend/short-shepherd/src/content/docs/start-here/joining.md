So you want in? Great — here's how it works.

:::caution[Read the rules first]
Go read the [Network Rules](/rules/network-rules/) before you do anything
else. Admins are instructed to **not reply** to you if you try to join without
acknowledging them first.
:::

## Age verification

Every space in the network is 18+, so we require some form of documentation to
enter. We do **not** save your documentation in any way, nor would we ever
want to. The only way documentation remains visible to us is if you decide not
to delete it in a chat yourself.

### Accepted forms of ID

| ID                                              |
| :---------------------------------------------- |
| A driving license that shows your date of birth |
| A national ID that shows your date of birth     |

## The steps

1. Request to join a chat through the join button. Our wiki bot will DM you
   this checklist right away, so you have everything at hand.
2. Talk to any of our admins — the [staff page](/community/staff/) shows who
   they are and **who can verify ID**.
3. Make sure you tell them you are seeking to join a specific chat.
4. Send your ID (see [accepted forms of ID](#accepted-forms-of-id) above).
5. Wait for instructions from the admin. You may be accepted into the group or
   asked for some further proof of documentation.
6. Once you're in, you will **not** be required to send your ID again when
   joining other groups in the network.

:::tip[One-time thing]
Verification carries across the whole network. Do it once, and every other
chat and channel opens up without repeating the process.
:::
