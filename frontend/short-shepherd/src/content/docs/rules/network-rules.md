platform. Moderators may act at their own discretion; when in doubt, ask
before posting.

## How the Rules Are Written

Every rule has a permanent number (§ 1, § 2, …) and is broken down into
numbered clauses (§ 1.1, § 1.2, …), each with its own heading. A clause is
the unit you get warned or banned under: staff cite the clause, not the
rule.

The rules follow an **amendment style**: nothing is silently edited. When a
rule needs to say more, a new clause is **added to that rule** with the next
free number. When a clause needs to change, it is reworded in place and the
change is recorded in the [Amendment Log](#amendment-log). Clause numbers are
never reused, and a clause that stops applying is kept, struck through, so
the history stays readable.

Occasionally a clause in one rule touches another rule. When that happens it
says so in its text ("see § 4.2"), so you can always follow the pointer.

Where a clause exists because of something that happened, it says so:
"_Introduced because of incident …_" links to the
[incident record](/incidents/).

Some rules carry a marker under their title:

- <span class="cs-flag cs-flag-hot">💥 Frequently broken</span> marks a rule
  that gets broken a lot, usually by accident. Read those twice.

## The Rules

### <span class="cs-num">1</span> <span class="cs-title">18+ Only</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">1.1</span> Age Requirement

You must be 18 or over. Every space in the network is adult in nature, even the ones that are safe for work.

#### <span class="cs-num">1.2</span> Verification Required

Complete [the joining process](/start-here/joining/), including age verification, before entering any chat.

#### <span class="cs-num">1.3</span> Attestation

You may be let in without ID if a trusted member attests to staff that you are 18 or over. If that turns out false, the attester is treated as having broken this rule too.

#### <span class="cs-num">1.4</span> Network-Wide Verification

Verification is done once and is valid in every chat of the network.

#### <span class="cs-num">1.5</span> Accepted Forms of ID

A driving licence or a national ID that shows your date of birth. Staff do
not store your documentation; delete it from the chat yourself once you are
verified.

### <span class="cs-num">2</span> <span class="cs-title">No Discrimination</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">2.1</span> No Discrimination

Discrimination in any shape or form is not tolerated.

#### <span class="cs-num">2.2</span> No Slurs

Racial slurs are not allowed, in any context.

#### <span class="cs-num">2.3</span> Jokes at Admin Discretion

Jokes are not a loophole. Admins decide what is a joke and what is discrimination, and act accordingly.

#### <span class="cs-num">2.4</span> Drop the Topic When Asked

If an admin asks you to drop the topic at hand, drop it, even if you believe no line was crossed.

#### <span class="cs-num">2.5</span> Ethnicity

Discrimination based on ethnicity is explicitly prohibited, not only discrimination based on race. Grouping people by their ethnic background, or judging anybody on the basis of it, is banned.
_Introduced because of [incident 260819](/incidents/260819/)._

#### <span class="cs-num">2.6</span> Applies to Every Group

This applies no matter which ethnic group is being talked about. Claims like "white people can't experience racism" break this rule: they judge a person by the group they were born into.
_Introduced because of [incident 260819](/incidents/260819/)._

:::note[For the record]
None of the owners of this network is ethnically white. These clauses are not
written to protect ourselves; they are written so that they protect everyone.
They are also not commentary on historical events or social issues. They say
nothing about the world outside; they only say how people are treated in
here.
:::

### <span class="cs-num">3</span> <span class="cs-title">Do Not Incite Violence</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">3.1</span> No Incitement

Do not call for, threaten, or glorify violence against anyone, inside or outside the network. Disagree as hard as you like; leave violence out of it.

### <span class="cs-num">4</span> <span class="cs-title">No Drama</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">4.1</span> No Provocation

Do not create drama, provoke other members, or make a scene.

#### <span class="cs-num">4.2</span> Report, Don't Fight

If someone is causing you harm, do not fight it out in the chat. Report it to the [staff](/community/staff/). Your report becomes a ticket handled by one assigned admin, as described in the [Moderation Strategy](/moderation/strategy/#handling-a-ticket).

#### <span class="cs-num">4.3</span> Reporting Staff

Staff are held to these rules too. If a member of staff is the problem, do not argue with them in the chat: report them to a different admin, or to someone above their role, via the [staff page](/community/staff/).

#### <span class="cs-num">4.4</span> Remain Civil

When somebody disagrees with you, your first reaction must not be "fuck off", "shut up", or anything in that family. Being right does not earn a pass on how you say it; neither does being upset.
_Introduced because of [incident 260819](/incidents/260819/)._

#### <span class="cs-num">4.5</span> Once, Then Away

Explain yourself once, calmly, or step away. If the other person will not let it go, report it under § 4.2 instead of escalating. This applies to members and staff alike.
_Introduced because of [incident 260819](/incidents/260819/)._

#### <span class="cs-num">4.6</span> Discussion Is Allowed

No drama does not mean no discussion. Disagreements are part of talking, and civil discussion, debate included, is welcome as long as it stays within these rules. This rule targets how people argue, not whether they do.

### <span class="cs-num">5</span> <span class="cs-title">No Doxxing</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">5.1</span> No Personal Information

Do not share, or threaten to share, anybody's personal information without their consent: legal name, address, workplace, phone number, photos, or accounts they keep separate from their sona.

#### <span class="cs-num">5.2</span> No Exceptions

It does not matter who the target is, what they did, whether they are still here, or whether the information is "already out there". Doxxing can be a crime and exposes the doxxer and the people running the chat to legal consequences.

#### <span class="cs-num">5.3</span> Ban on First Offence

Doxxing is a ban on the first offence.

#### <span class="cs-num">5.4</span> Safety Concerns Go to Staff

If someone's safety is genuinely at stake, tell the [staff](/community/staff/) privately instead of posting.

### <span class="cs-num">6</span> <span class="cs-title">Speak as Yourself</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">6.1</span> Post From Your Own Account

Do not use Telegram's "post as channel" feature in any chat of the network. A channel cannot be moderated like a person and makes dodging a mute trivial.

#### <span class="cs-num">6.2</span> Channel Posts Are Removed

Messages posted as a channel may be deleted without warning. Doing it repeatedly is treated as moderation evasion.

#### <span class="cs-num">6.3</span> Forward Instead

To share something from your channel, forward the post from your own account.

### <span class="cs-num">7</span> <span class="cs-title">Don't Ask for Special Privileges</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">7.1</span> No Requests for Privileges

Do not ask for admin powers, a moderator badge, or any other special treatment. They are not handed out on request.

#### <span class="cs-num">7.2</span> Apply Through Staff

If you want to help run the place, reach out to an owner or an admin via the [staff page](/community/staff/).

### <span class="cs-num">8</span> <span class="cs-title">No Revolving Door</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">8.1</span> No Join and Leave Cycling

Do not join and leave repeatedly. It floods the chat with service messages.

#### <span class="cs-num">8.2</span> Three Strikes

After 3 iterations, you will be banned.

### <span class="cs-num">9</span> <span class="cs-title">No Unapproved Advertising</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">9.1</span> Approval Required

Do not advertise without permission from an admin or mod.

#### <span class="cs-num">9.2</span> Approval Is Per Post

Permission once is not permission forever. Every ad is approved beforehand.

#### <span class="cs-num">9.3</span> What Counts as Advertising

Any post meant to send people elsewhere or get them to spend money: invites to other groups or servers, commissions, adoptables, YCHs, paid or subscription platforms, vote/boost/share requests, and promotion on behalf of others.

#### <span class="cs-num">9.4</span> What Does Not Count

Sharing your own art, saying where to follow you, sourcing somebody else's work, or answering a direct question. See the [FAQ](/community/faq/#what-counts-as-advertising) for examples. Unsure? [Ask a mod](/community/staff/).

### <span class="cs-num">10</span> <span class="cs-title">Don't Spam</span>

<span class="cs-flag cs-flag-hot" title="This rule is broken often">💥 Frequently broken</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">10.1</span> Album Limit

Albums are allowed up to 10 images.

#### <span class="cs-num">10.2</span> Let the Chat Breathe

There is no set cooldown, but do not flood. Give others room to talk between your posts.

#### <span class="cs-num">10.3</span> No Image-Only Posting

Never speaking and only posting images is also spam.

### <span class="cs-num">11</span> <span class="cs-title">Spoiler Real-Life NSFW</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">11.1</span> Spoiler Required

Real-life NSFW content is allowed only when spoilered: images, videos, gifs, and anything suggestive or lewd.

#### <span class="cs-num">11.2</span> Ask Before Posting

If you are not sure whether something needs a spoiler, ask an admin before posting it.

### <span class="cs-num">12</span> <span class="cs-title">Politics, Carefully</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">12.1</span> Respectful Wording

Political discussion is allowed as long as the wording is respectful and members exercise caution around anything that may be uncomfortable. Anything banned elsewhere in these rules stays banned in a political discussion.

#### <span class="cs-num">12.2</span> Admin Shutdown

Admins can shut down a political discussion at their discretion. When they do, it is over.

### <span class="cs-num">13</span> <span class="cs-title">Respect the Controlled-Content Lists</span>

<span class="cs-flag cs-flag-hot" title="This rule is broken often">💥 Frequently broken</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">13.1</span> Artists

Do not post work by an artist on the [banned list](/controlled/), stickers
included.

#### <span class="cs-num">13.2</span> Characters

Do not post art in which a [banned character](/controlled/) appears, stickers
included, no matter who drew it.

#### <span class="cs-num">13.3</span> Tags

Do not post content featuring a [banned kink or tag](/controlled/).

#### <span class="cs-num">13.4</span> Spoiler Controlled Content

Content on the [controlled list](/controlled/) may only be posted behind a
spoiler, with a content warning. How the lists work is explained in the
[Controlled Content policy](/rules/controlled-content/).

### <span class="cs-num">14</span> <span class="cs-title">No Paysite Leaks</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">14.1</span> No Leaks

Do not share paysite content unless it has been publicly posted somewhere.

#### <span class="cs-num">14.2</span> Artist Permission

Sharing with the artist's permission is fine.

### <span class="cs-num">15</span> <span class="cs-title">No AI "Art"</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">15.1</span> No AI Images

AI-generated images are not allowed anywhere in the network, spoilered or not.

### <span class="cs-num">16</span> <span class="cs-title">Repeat Offenders</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">16.1</span> Escalating Consequences

Individuals with enough complaints against them may be kicked, timed out, or banned from the chats and the network.

### <span class="cs-num">17</span> <span class="cs-title">Limited Ex Post Facto</span>

#### <span class="cs-num">17.1</span> No Retroactive Rules

Rules apply from the moment they are adopted. Nobody is warned, muted or
banned for something that was allowed when they did it.

#### <span class="cs-num">17.2</span> Closed Means Closed

A closed case is not reopened, and the same facts are not judged twice. A
new rule or clause does not reopen cases closed before it.

#### <span class="cs-num">17.3</span> New Offences

If offences that were not known at the time come to light after a case is
closed, they form a new case. Only the new facts are judged. This is rare
and is meant to stay rare.
