---
title: Network Rules
description: The rules that apply across every chat and channel in the CatShark TreeHouse network.
---

These rules apply in **every** chat and channel of the network, on every
platform. Moderators may act at their own discretion — when in doubt, ask
before posting.

1. **18+ only.** You must be 18 or over to join. To join, please refer to
   [the joining process](/start-here/joining/).
2. **No discrimination.** Discrimination in any shape or form is not
   tolerated. Do not use racial slurs in the chat. Admins know how to tell
   jokes and discrimination apart, but may take action at their own
   discretion.
3. **Do not incite violence.**
4. **No drama.** Do not create drama, provoke other members, or make a scene.
   If another individual is causing you harm, contact one of the
   [staff](/community/staff/).
5. **No doxxing.** It is extremely forbidden to dox others.
6. **Speak as yourself.** Do not speak as a channel — use your actual account.
7. **Don't ask for special privileges.** If you want to apply to staff, reach
   out to the owner or an admin via the [staff page](/community/staff/).
8. **No revolving door.** It is forbidden to join and leave repeatedly. After
   3 iterations, you will be banned.
9. **No unapproved advertising.** Do not advertise without permission from an
   admin or mod. Permission once does not mean permission forever — every ad
   must be approved beforehand.
10. **Don't spam.** Albums are allowed up to 10 images. There is no set
    cooldown, but let the chat breathe. Never speaking and _only_ posting
    images is also considered spam.
11. **Spoiler real-life NSFW.** Real-life NSFW content is allowed only when
    spoilered — images, videos, gifs, and anything that may be considered
    suggestive or lewd. Consult an admin if unsure.
12. **Politics, carefully.** Political discussions are allowed with a couple
    of exceptions, as long as the wording is respectful and members exercise
    caution around anything that may be uncomfortable. **Admins can shut down
    a discussion at their discretion.**
13. **Respect the controlled-content lists.** Refrain from posting any images
    or art containing [banned kinks or banned artists](/controlled/). See the
    [Controlled Content policy](/rules/controlled-content/) for how the lists
    work.
14. **No paysite leaks.** Sharing paysite content is forbidden unless it is
    publicly posted somewhere. Sharing such art with the artist's permission
    is fine.
15. **No AI "art".**
16. **Repeat offenders.** Individuals with enough complaints against their
    persona may find themselves kicked, timed out, or banned from the chats
    and the network.
