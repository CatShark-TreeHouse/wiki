Some artists, characters, and kinks are **controlled** in the network: either
banned outright or required to be spoilered. This page explains the policy;
the actual entries live on the **[Banned & Spoilered page](/controlled/)**,
which reads the current lists directly from the moderation database.

## The two statuses

| Status        | What it means                                                                                               |
| :------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Banned**    | Not allowed in the network, period. This **includes stickers** and, for characters, any art they appear in. |
| **Spoilered** | Allowed, but it **must** be sent spoilered and with a content warning.                                      |

## What can be controlled

- **Artists** — banned artists include their stickers. You can assume any
  artist that draws cub or zoo content is banned, whether or not they're on
  the list yet.
- **Characters** — a banned character covers stickers and any piece of art
  they appear in. Aliases of the same character are listed individually.
- **Kinks** (tags) — banned kinks may not be posted at all; spoilered kinks
  must carry a content warning and be spoilered when sent.

## How the lists are maintained

Moderators manage the lists through the network's wiki bot — entries are
added with a single-word alias (for example `NozomyArts`) plus an optional
reason. The [live page](/controlled/) always shows the current state; there
is no separate document to keep in sync.

You can also ask the bot directly in chat whether something is controlled
with `/check <name>`.

:::note[Not sure about something?]
If you're unsure whether a piece of art, an artist, or a kink is okay to
post, ask one of the [staff](/community/staff/) first. That's always the
safe move.
:::
