---
title: Incidents
description: "A public record of significant incidents in the network: what happened, what the team did, and what changed because of it."
---

When something goes seriously wrong in the network (a ban, a rule change, or
a loss of trust in the team) it gets an incident record. Each record is a
plain, third-person account written after the fact: what happened, how the
team handled it, what the outcome was, and what changed as a result.

Incident records are **not** the full internal report. They do not reproduce
chat logs or screenshots, and they describe people by their role in the
events rather than by name. Their purpose is accountability: members should
be able to see why a rule exists and how the team has handled, and sometimes
mishandled, past situations.

## Severity

| Severity   | Meaning                                             |
| :--------- | :-------------------------------------------------- |
| **Low**    | Resolved in-chat; nothing beyond a warning.         |
| **Medium** | Required admin action and/or a member left.         |
| **High**   | Bans, rule changes, or damage to trust in the team. |

## Status

| Status         | Meaning                           |
| :------------- | :-------------------------------- |
| **Open**       | Still being handled.              |
| **Monitoring** | Handled; watching for recurrence. |
| **Closed**     | No further action expected.       |
