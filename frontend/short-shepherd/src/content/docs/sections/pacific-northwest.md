**PNW Furs** is a small side chat of the network for furs in the Pacific
Northwest.

:::caution[Same Rules Apply]
All the [Network Rules](/rules/network-rules/) apply in the Pacific Northwest
section exactly as they do everywhere else in the network, including
[18+ only](/rules/network-rules/#1-18-only) and the
[Banned & Controlled lists](/controlled/). Being a regional chat changes
nothing.
:::
