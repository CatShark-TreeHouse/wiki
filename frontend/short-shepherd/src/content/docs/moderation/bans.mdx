This is the register of people banned from the network. It exists so that a
ban is a matter of record, not rumour: who, when, and, where the team chooses
to state it, why. A reason is optional; some bans are better left
unexplained in public, and the absence of a reason is not an invitation to
guess one. The **Ban** column reads `Permanent` or `Until(date)`; the
**Date** column is when it started. Temporary bans are removed from the
register once they expire. **Extra detail** links to static evidence
(usually a screenshot) where the team has chosen to publish it.

Bans are decided under the [Network Rules](/rules/network-rules/) and
handled as described in the [Moderation Strategy](/moderation/strategy/).
Where a ban came out of a recorded incident, the incident is linked. Entries
are added through pull requests, like everything else on this wiki, so every
addition and removal has a visible history.

## Appeals

A ban can be appealed to the owners, who act as the
[judicial branch](/moderation/strategy/#separation-of-powers). An appeal is
heard once; new facts may reopen it under
[§ 17.3](/rules/network-rules/#173-new-offenses). Contact an owner via the
[Staff page](/community/staff/).
