## Supremacy Clause

The [Network Rules](/rules/network-rules/) are the highest authority in the
CatShark TreeHouse network. They apply in every chat, channel and server that
belongs to the network, on every platform, and they override anything that
conflicts with them.

In practice:

1. **Network over chat.** Any individual chat may add its own rules, but only
   to be stricter or more specific. A chat rule that contradicts a network
   rule is void, and the network rule applies.
2. **Later over earlier.** The rules are written in amendment style. When two
   network rules conflict, the later rule prevails, and it must say which
   earlier rule it overrides or extends.
3. **Rules over staff.** Staff act under the rules, not above them. A
   moderation action that has no basis in the rules can be appealed to the
   owners and undone.
4. **Owners over the rest.** Where the rules are silent or unclear, the
   owners decide. Their decision stands until it is written into the rules as
   an amendment.
5. **Higher role over lower role.** Within the staff, authority follows the
   hierarchy: Owner over Main Admin, Main Admin over Admin, Admin over
   Moderator. A higher role may overrule, pause or reverse a decision made by
   a lower one, and a lower role may not overrule a higher one. Disagreements
   go up the chain, never into the member chat.

### Separation of Powers

<PowersDiagram />

The staff is organized in three branches, loosely modelled on a government:

| Branch          | Who                                                              | What they do                                                                                                          |
| :-------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legislative** | Owners, every Main Admin, and the Admins of the Moderation group | Write, amend and repeal the rules. No rule change is valid unless it comes out of this body.                          |
| **Executive**   | The Moderators of the Moderation group                           | Apply the rules in the chats day to day: warnings, mutes, removals, and the live handling of incidents.               |
| **Judicial**    | Owners, when needed                                              | Hear appeals, settle disputes over what a rule means, and review executive actions. Their reading of a rule is final. |

The branches are kept apart on purpose. The people who write a rule are not
the ones who enforce it in the moment, and the people who enforce it are not
the ones who judge whether it was enforced correctly.

### Limited Ex Post Facto

Rules are never applied backwards, and closed cases stay closed; only
offenses discovered after the fact can open a new case, and rarely. This is
written into the rules as
[§ 17](/rules/network-rules/#17-limited-ex-post-facto), so that members and
staff can hold the team to it.

## Telegram Chat Moderation

Moderation of a Telegram chat is divided into **roles**. Each role is
accountable to the one above it, so every person knows exactly who they
coordinate with.

### Roles

| Role           | Scope                                                                           |
| :------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Owner**      | The network as a whole.                                                         |
| **Main Admin** | Leads one coordination group (Moderation, Events, …) and answers to the owners. |
| **Admin**      | Runs part of a coordination group under its Main Admin.                         |
| **Moderator**  | Day-to-day moderation within the scope of their Admin.                          |

### Structure

```
Owners
└── Main Admin (one per coordination group)
    └── Admins
        └── Moderators
```

### Handling a Ticket

Every report, complaint or incident is a **ticket**, whether it arrives by
direct message, in the chat, or is spotted by staff. This is the other half
of [§ 4.2](/rules/network-rules/#42-report-dont-fight): members are asked not to fight it out in public, and in return the team promises to handle things
quietly and in order.

1. **One admin is assigned.** A ticket is picked up by exactly one admin, who
   owns it from start to finish. They are the only staff member who acts on
   it in the member chat.
2. **Supporting admins, if necessary.** The assigned admin may bring in
   others for a second opinion, for context, or because the situation is
   bigger than one person. Supporting admins work through the assigned
   admin, in the staff chat, and do not step into the member chat on that
   ticket unless asked to.
3. **Nobody else joins in.** Staff who are not assigned stay out of the
   situation in the member chat, even if they have an opinion about it. If
   they disagree with how it is being handled, that goes up the hierarchy,
   not into the chat.
4. **Topics that have died stay dead.** If a situation has already calmed
   down on its own, the ticket is handled in private, not by reopening it in
   the chat.
5. **Private matters stay private.** Warnings, deletion requests and anything
   about a specific member are handled in direct messages.
6. **No rule changes mid-ticket.** If a ticket shows that a rule is missing
   or unclear, that is noted and taken to the legislative body afterwards.
   The ticket itself is handled under the rules as they stand.
7. **Close and record.** The ticket is closed by the assigned admin. Anything
   significant enough becomes an [incident record](/incidents/).
