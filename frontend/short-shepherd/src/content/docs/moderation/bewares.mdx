Most of what staff can moderate happens in the chats. A lot of what actually
hurts people happens in private messages, where nobody else can see it. This
page is for that gap: a short list of people whose behaviour pattern the team
has seen evidence of and thinks you should know about before you find out
the hard way.

Being on this list is not a ban, though some people on it are also banned.
It is a warning, with the evidence the team based it on, so you can judge for
yourself. Entries are added only by the owners, only with evidence, and only
for patterns of behaviour, not for a single bad day. If you believe an entry
is wrong, contact an [owner](/community/staff/).

If someone is doing this to you right now: you can stop replying. You do not
owe anyone a reason for leaving a conversation. Forward the messages to a
[member of staff](/community/staff/) if you want them recorded
([§ 4.2](/rules/network-rules/#42-report-dont-fight)).
