description: How the CatShark TreeHouse staff is organized — the four roles and the four teams.
---

The network divides all staff responsibilities into two different scopes:
**roles** (how much and what kind of authority someone holds) and **teams**
(what area they specialize in).

## Roles

| Role          | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Owner**     | The single highest point of contact and responsibility of the network. The owner must try to appeal to all the admins and mods alike, while keeping the experience of the network as enjoyable as possible. Under emergencies, the owner coordinates all other admins and makes the final decisions. Under any other circumstances, the owner is no different than a polymorph. |
| **Admin**     | The main points of contact for the owner — one tier under in hierarchy, logically, but not functionally. Admins have complete freedom over their actions as they deem necessary, but may be subject to reactions from other admins, mods, polymorphs, and the owner. They are oftentimes team leaders.                                                                          |
| **Mod**       | Most of the staff. Mods hold the same powers as admins, but are scoped to a specific platform, team, or group. They hold the most responsibility in a scope because they hold the most **context**. Mods are not _under_ admins — they are a sibling tier acting within a more specific context.                                                                                |
| **Polymorph** | The most versatile of all the roles, and the least specialized as a consequence. Polymorphs help wherever they're able, often have unusual responsibilities, or run their own chats and networks. Not a default role like Mod or Admin — an evolution of how help has been needed over time.                                                                                    |

## Teams

Teams define a scope for different members of the staff. Teams are directed or
coordinated by admins (and the owner when needed); mods specialize in their
team, and admins act as coordinators between their team and others.

| Team                    | Responsibilities                                    | What it's for                                                                                                                               |
| :---------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Network Logistics**   | Internal roles, processes, approvals, effectiveness | As the network grows, internal management becomes necessary: controlling internal processes, recognizing problems, and solving them.        |
| **Moderation**          | Chat moderation                                     | Brings order and well-being to the chats in ways that are fair and justified, acting on the rules each chat presents.                       |
| **Member Satisfaction** | Long-term events, chat feeling                      | Creates the long-term events that make the chats unique — group art pieces, games, large-scale events that keep the community alive.        |
| **Member Engagement**   | Short-term events, spontaneous                      | Manages short-lived, recurring, or spontaneous events — movie nights, game nights. Want to suggest an event? Contact a member of this team. |
