The network divides all staff responsibilities into two different scopes:
**roles** (how much and what kind of authority someone holds) and **teams**
(what area they specialize in).

## Roles

Roles form a strict hierarchy: each role answers to the one above it. The
full structure and the reasoning behind it are on the
[Moderation Strategy page](/moderation/strategy/).

| Role           | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**      | The highest point of contact and responsibility. The owners must appeal to staff and members alike while keeping the network enjoyable. Under emergencies they coordinate everyone else and make the final decisions. |
| **Main Admin** | Leads one team and is its point of contact for the owners. Coordinates the admins of their team and represents it towards the other teams.                                                                            |
| **Admin**      | Runs part of a team under its Main Admin. Admins act with freedom within their scope, but may be subject to reactions from their Main Admin and the owners.                                                           |
| **Moderator**  | Most of the staff. Moderators hold the day-to-day powers within the scope of their Admin (a specific platform, chat, or team) and hold the most responsibility there because they hold the most **context**.          |

## Teams

Teams (also called coordination groups) define a scope for different members
of the staff. Each team is led by its Main Admin; admins run parts of it and
moderators specialize within it. Main Admins act as coordinators between their
team and the others, and the owners step in when needed.

| Team                    | Responsibilities                                    | What it's for                                                                                                                              |
| :---------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Network Logistics**   | Internal roles, processes, approvals, effectiveness | As the network grows, internal management becomes necessary: controlling internal processes, recognizing problems, and solving them.       |
| **Moderation**          | Chat moderation                                     | Brings order and well-being to the chats in ways that are fair and justified, acting on the rules each chat presents.                      |
| **Member Satisfaction** | Long-term events, chat feeling                      | Creates the long-term events that make the chats unique: group art pieces, games, large-scale events that keep the community alive.        |
| **Member Engagement**   | Short-term events, spontaneous                      | Manages short-lived, recurring, or spontaneous events: movie nights, game nights. Want to suggest an event? Contact a member of this team. |
