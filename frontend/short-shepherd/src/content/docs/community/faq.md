## What Counts as Advertising?

[§ 9](/rules/network-rules/#9-no-unapproved-advertising) says advertising needs approval from a moderator first. Advertising is any post whose
purpose is to send people somewhere else or to get them to spend money.

**Needs approval:**

| Example                                                             | Why it counts                        |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------- |
| "Come join my Telegram group / Discord server: t.me/…"              | Invite to another community          |
| "Commissions open! 3 slots, $40 for a sketch"                       | Sale                                 |
| "Adoptable batch, DM to claim" or "YCH, bids start at $20"          | Sale                                 |
| "Support me on Patreon / Ko-fi / Boosty / Fansly for exclusive art" | Paid or subscription platform        |
| "Please vote for my entry / boost my server / retweet this"         | Asking people to promote something   |
| "My friend opened commissions, here is their sheet"                 | Promotion on behalf of somebody else |
| Posting the same link in several network chats                      | Cross-posting a promotion            |

**Does not need approval:**

| Example                                                              | Why it is fine                     |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- |
| Posting a piece you drew, with no sales pitch attached               | Sharing your own art               |
| "You can find me on Bluesky / FA / Twitter as @…"                    | Telling people where to follow you |
| Posting somebody else's art with a link to the source                | Crediting the artist               |
| Answering "where can I commission you?" when somebody asks           | Replying to a direct question      |
| Linking a wiki, tutorial, or tool that is useful to the conversation | Not promotion                      |

Unsure which side a post lands on? [Ask a moderator](/community/staff/) before
posting it. Permission is per post, not forever.

## How Do the Banned & Controlled Lists Get Updated?

The moderation team edits them through pull requests on
[GitHub](https://github.com/CatShark-TreeHouse/wiki); the
[Banned & Controlled page](/controlled/) is rebuilt from that data on every
merge. If you think an entry is missing or wrong, tell a
[member of staff](/community/staff/).

## What Are Roles and Teams?

The staff structure is explained on the
[Roles & Teams page](/community/roles-and-teams/): owners, main admins,
admins, and moderators, plus the four teams they organize into.

## How Can I Join the Staff?

Ask an [admin or an owner](/community/staff/)!

## Who Do I Contact if Someone Is Causing Problems?

Any of the [staff](/community/staff/); do not fight it out in the chat
([§ 4.2](/rules/network-rules/#42-report-dont-fight)). If the person causing problems is a member of staff, report them to
a different admin or to someone above their role ([§ 4.3](/rules/network-rules/#43-reporting-staff)).
