## How do I join?

Head to [the joining process](/start-here/joining/). We only accept people
over the age of 18 — see [accepted forms of
ID](/start-here/joining/#accepted-forms-of-id).

## Which chats and channels belong to the network?

The full table lives on the [Welcome page](/start-here/welcome/#chats--channels-in-the-network).

## What can't I post?

Read the [Network Rules](/rules/network-rules/) for conduct, and the
[Controlled Content policy](/rules/controlled-content/) for banned and
spoilered artists, characters, and kinks. The current entries are always on
the [live Banned & Spoilered page](/controlled/).

## How do the banned/spoilered lists get updated?

Moderators maintain them through the network's wiki bot; the
[live page](/controlled/) reads directly from the same database, so it is
never out of date. In chat, you can ask the bot yourself with
`/check <name>`.

## What are roles and teams?

The staff structure is explained on the
[Roles & Teams page](/community/roles-and-teams/) — the owner, admins, mods,
and polymorphs, plus the four teams they organize into.

## How can I join the staff?

Ask an [admin or the owner](/community/staff/)!

## Who do I contact if someone is causing problems?

Any of the [staff](/community/staff/) — moderation actions in chat are
handled by the moderation team and its tooling.
