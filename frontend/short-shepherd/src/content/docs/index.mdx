## How this wiki stays current

The rules pages you read here are versioned in the open on
[GitHub](https://github.com/CatShark-TreeHouse/wiki). The
[Banned & Spoilered lists](/controlled/) are different: moderators maintain
them through the network's wiki bot, and this site reads them live — what you
see is always the current state, not a snapshot.
